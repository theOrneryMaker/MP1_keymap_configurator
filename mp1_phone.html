MP1

Status
Not connected

Plug the macropad into the phone (USB-C cable or an OTG adapter), then Connect… and pick it. It has two ports: if the one you pick is its serial console, the page says so and you pick the other.

Firmware update

Pick an MP1_firmware_NN.zip (from Downloads, Drive, email…). It's checked first; your keymap, settings, wiring and layers are never touched.

Backup

Saves the macropad's own files (keymap, settings, wiring, layers, profiles, keys) into one .json on the phone.
