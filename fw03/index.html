MP1 Firmware 03

For macropads on firmware 03. Pads still on the original firmware keep using the old configurator.

Pages

  • MP1: configurator, help, workbench and module guide.
  • MP1 Link: the macropad's screen live, layer and profile switching.
  • MP1 Phone: firmware update and backup from an Android phone.

Use Chrome or Edge. The pages talk to the macropad over USB.

Downloads

  • MP1_firmware_03.zip: for pads on standard CircuitPython.
  • MP1_firmware_03_custom.zip: for pads on the MP1 custom firmware.
  • mp1_yd_rp2040_03.uf2: the MP1 custom firmware itself (YD-RP2040), copied onto the RPI-RP2 drive.

Updates never include your keymap, settings or wiring.
